How to Reach Oviedo

Airport, train, and local transport options

By Plane

The closest airport is Asturias Airport (OVD), located about 45 km from Oviedo.

  • Shuttle Bus: The ALSA bus service runs every 30–60 minutes from the airport to the Oviedo Bus Station. The journey takes approximately 45 minutes and costs around €9.
  • Taxi: Available at the airport arrivals terminal. The fare to Oviedo city center is approximately €60–€75.

By Train from Madrid

Oviedo is connected via the Spanish high-speed rail network (AVE).

  • Main Station: Oviedo Railway Station (Uría Station).
  • Local Train: If your hotel is near the venue, you can transfer to a local train and stop at the Estación de Llamaquique, which is only an 8-minute walk from the School.

Local Transport in Oviedo

Oviedo is a very walkable city. If you need public transport, city bus line 11 stops just 2 minutes from the venue at González Besada street.

Lines 2, 9, and 10 stop at Plaza de la Gesta, about a 5-minute walk from the School.

Alternative Airports

Madrid-Barajas, Santander, and Bilbao are practical alternatives when Asturias Airport is not convenient.

Madrid-Barajas Airport (MAD)

Most common entry point for international travelers

  • By Train: Take the Cercanías train from Terminal 4 to Madrid-Chamartín Station, then take the high-speed AVE or Alvia train to Oviedo. The journey from Chamartín takes approximately 3h 15m to 3h 45m.
  • By Bus: ALSA operates direct buses from Madrid Airport Terminal 4 to Oviedo. The journey takes about 5 to 6 hours.

Santander Airport (SDR)

Popular for low-cost carrier connections

  • By Bus: Direct ALSA service from Santander Airport to Oviedo Bus Station. The trip takes roughly 2h 30m to 3h.
  • By Car: Straightforward 2-hour drive along the A-8 coastal highway.

Bilbao Airport (BIO)

Wider range of international connections

  • By Bus: Take the shuttle bus from the airport to Bilbao Intermodal. From there, several direct ALSA buses run to Oviedo daily.
  • Total travel time: About 4h 30m to 5h.

Accommodation Recommendations

Nearby options for workshop participants

Hotel Zentral Ramiro I

This is one of the most reliable options in Oviedo, specifically geared toward business travel, conferences, and academic events.

  • Vibe: Professional, functional, excellent business infrastructure, and very friendly staff.
  • Distance: Approximately a 5–8 minute walk to the School of Computer Science Engineering.
Official Website

Eurostars Palacio de Cristal

One of Oviedo's most iconic buildings, designed by Santiago Calatrava. It is located within the Llamaquique area, making it one of the closest high-end options to the workshop venue.

  • Vibe: Modern, architectural landmark, quiet area.
  • Distance: About a 10-minute walk to the School.
Official Website

Hotel Nature Oviedo

A polished, nature-inspired hotel located near the University campus area. It offers a great balance of comfort and proximity to the academic buildings.

  • Vibe: Relaxed, organic design, well-rated for business travel.
  • Distance: About a 12-minute walk to the School.
Official Website

AC Hotel Oviedo Forum

This hotel is located right next to the main train and bus stations, making it a good option if easy arrival and departure are the priority.

  • Vibe: Efficient, professional, close to the main shopping street, Calle Uría.
  • Distance: About a 20-minute walk or a 5-minute taxi/local train ride to the venue.
Official Website

Hotel Fruela

Located in the heart of the city, near the Cathedral and the historic Old Town. Ideal for those who want to explore Oviedo's famous Cider Boulevard in the evenings.

  • Vibe: Classic Oviedo, central, close to restaurants and historical sites.
  • Distance: About a 15-minute walk to the School.
Official Website
